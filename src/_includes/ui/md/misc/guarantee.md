<p class="lead">A money-back guarantee next to the price or the buy button: a seal with the number of days, a title and the terms in one sentence.</p>

## Technical Details

- It's an `aside` labelled by its title; the seal repeats the title, so it's hidden from screen readers.
- The seal uses the success alert color, the frame a dashed `border-strong` line, so it reads as a promise, not as another card.
- Only promise what you really offer, and keep the terms short and clear.
- Override the content with `guarantee` (`{ days, title, text }`).
