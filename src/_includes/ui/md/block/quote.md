<p class="lead">One big quote from a client or a customer, with their photo, name and role. Use it to let someone else tell the result of a project.</p>

## Technical Details

- The quote is a `figure` with a `blockquote` and a `figcaption` for the person, so the source belongs to the quote. The block has a visually hidden `h2` ("What the client says").
- The quote mark is decorative, and the photo has an empty `alt` because the name is next to it.
- One quote, written in the client's own words, is stronger than a carousel of many.
- Override the content with `quoteBlock`.
