## Technical Details

- The block is labelled by its `h2` (the greeting with the name); the eyebrow and the role around it are a stacked text group.
- The credentials are a description list shown value first (`column-reverse`), so they're read in a natural order.
- The photo has a meaningful `alt`; the offset frame behind it is a decorative pseudo-element that mirrors in RTL.
- Keep the bio personal and short: why you made it, and why the reader should trust you.
