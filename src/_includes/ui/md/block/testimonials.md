<p class="lead">Social proof in one block: a heading, an overall rating, testimonial cards and a logo cloud.</p>

## Technical Details

- It combines the [Block Heading](/ui/content-and-layout/block-heading/), [Testimonial Card](/ui/card/testimonial/) and [Logo Cloud](/ui/content-and-layout/logo-cloud/) components.
- The stars are decorative; the rating is written out in text ("4.9 out of 5 from 2,000+ reviews").
- Override the copy with `testimonialsBlock` and the logos with a `logoCloud` object.
