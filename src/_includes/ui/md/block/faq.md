<p class="lead">A frequently asked questions block with a sticky heading, a help card and an exclusive accordion.</p>

## Technical Details

- The questions are native `details` elements (styled by Spruce CSS) with the same `name`, so only one is open at a time; no JavaScript.
- The heading is the start-aligned [Block Heading](/ui/content-and-layout/block-heading/); on large screens the side column is sticky.
- Override the content with `faqBlock` (`heading`, `items` with `question` and `answer`, and `help`).
