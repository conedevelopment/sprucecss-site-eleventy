## Technical Details

- It's a list labelled "Why shop with us"; the icons are decorative, the title and the text carry the meaning.
- The items wrap with an auto-fit grid: four in a row on wide screens, one or two on phones.
- Keep the promises short and true; link them to the shipping and returns pages when you have them.
- Override the items with `trustBar` (`[{ icon, title, text }]`); the icons come from the shared block icon partial.
