<p class="lead">A site footer with the logo and a short description, link columns, a theme switcher, the copyright line and social links.</p>

## Anatomy

- **`.site-footer`** is the `footer` element (the contentinfo landmark).
- **`.site-footer__top`** is a `nav` labelled "Footer", with columns generated by the [layout-grid](/docs/sass/mixins/#layout-grid) mixin (min. 14rem, they wrap on their own).
- **`.site-footer__column`** has a heading (`__title`) and a list of links (`__navigation`) labelled by it.
- **`.site-footer__bottom`** holds the [Theme Switcher Button](/ui/misc/theme-switcher-button/), the copyright (`__copyright`) and the social links (`__social`).

## Accessibility

- The column headings are `h2`: the footer is a top-level part of the page, so its sections don't belong under the last heading of the content.
- The social icons have visually hidden text ("GitHub"), and the group is labelled "Follow us". Each icon link is at least 2.75rem large, which is easy to tap.
- The current page in the link lists can be marked with `aria-current="page"`; it's highlighted in bold and the primary color.

## Technical Details

- The columns need no breakpoints: `layout-grid` fits as many as there's room for.
- The links use the text color and turn primary on hover, so the footer stays calm but usable.
